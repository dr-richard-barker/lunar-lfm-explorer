\section*{Data Availability}
The planetary crustal magnetic field datasets analyzed in this study are available through the NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Planetary Plasma Interactions (PPI) and Geosciences Nodes under DOIs \href{https://doi.org/10.17189/1520494}{10.17189/1520494}, \href{https://doi.org/10.17189/rk57-g992}{10.17189/rk57-g992}, and \href{https://doi.org/10.1029/2019JE005979}{10.1029/2019JE005979}. Processed landing site tables, comparative systematic review datasets, and interactive WebGL visualization assets are archived in Zenodo (DOI will be assigned upon publication) and hosted at \url{https://github.com/dr-richard-barker/lunar-mars-magnetic-biology}.

\section*{Code Availability}
All Python analysis scripts, processing pipelines, figure generation modules, and manuscript source files are open source under the MIT license at \url{https://github.com/dr-richard-barker/lunar-mars-magnetic-biology}.

\section*{Author Contributions}
R.B. and D.M.P. conceived the research and experimental framework. R.B., A.K.S., M.D., and K.B. developed the data processing pipeline, conducted the magnetobiology literature review, and prepared the figures and tables. R.B., C.S.B., and D.M.P. drafted the manuscript and formulated the comparative biological risk matrix. All authors reviewed, edited, and approved the final manuscript.
