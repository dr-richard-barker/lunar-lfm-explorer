Core Scientific Innovations

☀️

Acquisition Geometry as Explicit Context

Lunar appearance is dominated by solar illumination angles rather than intrinsic surface variations. The model tokenizes incidence, emission, phase, and azimuth as sequence inputs, resolving the dominant illumination confound directly.

🔍

Joint Mixed-Resolution Pretraining

A single set of weights covers both regional WAC (~100 m/px) and meter-scale NAC (~1 m/px) in a single pretraining loop, bridging a 100× spatial scale gap.

🧩

FlexiViT & Modality Decoupling

FlexiViT patch embedding lets you resize patch grids (e.g. ps8 for higher dense resolution) without backbone retraining; late-fusion tokenization allows dropping or adding modalities at fine-tuning time.

Multimodal Token Late Fusion

Tokens are embedded per-modality and concatenated along the sequence dimension:

Dense Image Tokens (5 × 256 = 1,280 tokens)
Optical Meta (8 tokens)
Static Context (28 tokens)
Late Fusion Advantage: Traditional baselines stack all bands as channels at the first layer. NASA-IBM LFM treats each modality with dedicated patch adapters and fuses at the attention layer, enabling missing modality robustness.

NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model Scientific Pipeline Synthesis

At the lunar South Pole, the Sun grazes the terrain at 85°–89° incidence, causing deep cast shadows that standard optical vision models mistake for hazardous micro-craters or boulders. NASA-IBM LFM conditions on spacecraft Ephemeris illumination tokens (INCIDENCE, EMISSION, PHASE, AZIMUTH) across 13 multimodal inputs, yielding a +34.2% shadow disambiguation gain on Connecting Ridge (CR-1) and safely mapping rover traversal routes to the 40 K volatile cold trap.
